# Welcome to Sebastian Zajac Courses Pages!

Welcome to the official web page of my WAT courses.

## About me

I am a theoretical physicist and Assistant Professor at Military University of Technology, where I conduct research at the intersection of quantum computing and modern data science.

I'm passionate about translating these "abstract" physics concepts into powerful tools for data-driven decisions. Whether in the classroom or the lab, my goal is to push the boundaries of what's possible at the frontier of physics and AI.
